Monero in Biscuit

Monero in Biscuit is Feather Wallet: the same wallet code, the same connections to nodes, the same tools. If you know Feather, you know this part of Biscuit already.

The basics

  • Receive on subaddresses, one per payer, and send by pasting an address.
  • Monero received can be spent after 10 confirmations, about 20 minutes. See Fees and confirmations.
  • Your wallet finds its transactions by synchronizing with a node. The node never learns your addresses or your balance.

The tools

Accounts and subaddresses
Separate balances inside one wallet, and the primary address.
Coin control
Choose which outputs to spend, freeze some, sweep others.
Pay to many
Up to 15 recipients in one transaction.
Payment proofs
Prove you paid someone, or that a transaction is yours.
View-only wallets
See incoming payments without being able to spend.
Hardware wallets
Ledger and Trezor.
Offline signing
Keys on a computer that never goes online.
Monero seeds
Polyseed, 25-word seeds, passphrases, dice, damaged seeds.
Advanced tools
Importing and broadcasting transactions, the address checker, and more.
