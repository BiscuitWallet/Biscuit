The Home tab

The Home tab gathers what isn’t your wallet: prices, a converter, our Journal and Monero’s crowdfunding. All of it comes from biscuitwallet.com, see Prices and other data.

What’s there

Prices
Monero, Bitcoin and Litecoin, in your currency.
Journal
Our essays, news and release notes, the same as on the website. The type and subtitle of each piece show next to its title; pinned pieces stay on top. Double-click one to read it in your browser.
Crowdfunding
The proposals of Monero’s Community Crowdfunding System that are raising funds, with their progress.
Converter
Converts between coins and currencies. Configure chooses which ones it lists.

Prices and the Journal show the moment Biscuit opens, from the last copy, then refresh quietly. In Tor mode, they wait for Tor to be ready.

Choosing what you see

Settings › Plugins turns each part on or off. With third-party data disabled, the Home tab stays empty.
