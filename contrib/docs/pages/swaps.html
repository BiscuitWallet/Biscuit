Swaps: which one?

Biscuit swaps your coins two ways, both from the Swap tab, neither with an account.

Exchange swapAtomic swap
PairsXMR, BTC, LTC, any directionBTC → XMR
WithAn exchange, found by TrocadorAn independent market maker
Who holds your coinsThe exchange, during the swapNobody: the protocol enforces it
SpeedMinutesAbout an hour; keep Biscuit open
Over TorNoYes
StatusStableBeta

Which one to use

  • For Litecoin, for selling Monero, or when you want it done in minutes: an exchange swap.
  • For Bitcoin to Monero without trusting anyone with your coins, or over Tor: an atomic swap.
