History

The History tab lists every payment of the coin chosen at the top, newest first. Ctrl+F shows a search bar.

Monero

Double-click a transaction for its details: amount, fee, destinations, inputs. Right-click for more:

  • Show details, and from there Copy Tx Secret Key for payment proofs.
  • Create Tx Proof, see Payment proofs.
  • Copy the amount, the date, the description or the transaction ID.
  • View on block explorer. Choose which one in Settings › Misc › Links.
  • Resend transaction and Remove from history, for a payment that failed, see A payment I sent failed.

Double-click the description column to write a note on a payment. Notes stay in your wallet file, they’re never sent. While the wallet synchronizes, the history may be incomplete.

Bitcoin and Litecoin

Each line shows the amount, confirmations and date for the selected wallet. Right-click for Copy transaction ID, View on block explorer and Speed up (raise the fee), see Fees and speeding up.

Exporting

Wallet › History › Export CSV saves your Monero history to a spreadsheet file, for your accounts or your taxes. The columns:

ColumnWhat it holds
blockHeightThe block that included the transaction
timestamp, dateWhen the block was mined
accountIndexThe account. A payment between two of your accounts shows twice.
directionin or out
balanceDeltaHow much your balance changed, fee included
amountThe amount sent or received, fee not included
feeThe network fee. For incoming payments, the sender paid it.
txidThe transaction ID
descriptionYour note
paymentIDUsually empty

Import descriptions from CSV reads your notes back from such a file, for example after restoring from seed.
