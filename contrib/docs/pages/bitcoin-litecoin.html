Bitcoin and Litecoin in Biscuit

Bitcoin and Litecoin share the same wallet code in Biscuit. They are light wallets: instead of downloading the blockchain, Biscuit asks Electrum servers about your addresses, so they’re ready in seconds.

Seeds and addresses

Bitcoin and Litecoin are optional: add them when you create your wallet, or later with the + next to the coins. Your first Bitcoin and Litecoin wallets come from one 12-word BIP39 seed, the same as your first Ethereum wallet if you added one. Biscuit uses native SegWit addresses and the standard paths, so the same seed opens your coins in Sparrow, Electrum or any BIP39 wallet:

CoinAddressesPath
Bitcoinbc1q…m/84’/0’/0’ (BIP84)
Litecoinltc1q…m/84’/2’/0’ (BIP84)

Coins on older address types of the same seed (starting with 1, 3, L or M) don’t show in Biscuit. Restore the seed in a wallet that reads those, and send them to your Biscuit address.

Several wallets per coin

You can keep more than one Bitcoin or Litecoin wallet inside the same Biscuit wallet, to keep savings apart from spending money for example. Each has its own name, its own seed and its own balance.

  • Add wallet, above the address, creates a new one (Create a new seed) or restores one (Restore from seed, 12 or 24 words, with an optional BIP39 passphrase).
  • Switch between them with the buttons above the address on Send and Receive.
  • Right-click a button to Rename it, Show seed or remove it, the first one included. Removing a wallet removes it from Biscuit only: with its seed, you can add it back at any time. When a coin has no wallet left, it leaves the tabs.

Back up the seed of each wallet you add: it isn’t the same as your first one. The seeds menu, in Wallet or behind the seed button of the status bar, shows them all.
