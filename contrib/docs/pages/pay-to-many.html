Pay to many

One Monero transaction can pay up to 15 addresses at once, with a single fee. Useful for salaries, refunds, or splitting a bill.

How

Choose Tools › Pay to many: the Pay to field on Send takes several lines. Write one recipient per line, as address, amount:

This sends 1 XMR to the first address and 0.5 XMR to the second. The Amount field shows the total. Click Send and check every line in the confirmation.

The limit

A Monero transaction has at most 16 outputs, and one of them is your change: that leaves 15 recipients. For more, send several transactions.
