Supported systems

Biscuit runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. Take the file for your system from the download page, verify it, then follow the page for your system.

Requirements

SystemVersionFiles
WindowsWindows 10 or later, 64-bitInstaller or portable zip
macOSmacOS 14 Sonoma or laterApple Silicon (M1 and later) or Intel
Linuxx86_64, glibc 2.31 or later (2.34 for atomic swaps)AppImage, .deb, .rpm, Arch package, .tar.gz

Linux on ARM, such as a Raspberry Pi, isn’t supported yet. Neither are phones: Biscuit is a desktop wallet.
